## About

I am an incoming **M.A.Sc. student** in Electrical and Computer Engineering at [Queen's University](https://www.queensu.ca/) (Sep 2026), supervised by [Dr. Ning Lu](https://www.queensu.ca/research/researchers/ning-lu) (Canada Research Chair, Tier 2 in Future Communication Networks). My research focuses on **federated learning over wireless networks** — designing communication-efficient, heterogeneity-robust, and privacy-preserving distributed ML systems.

Previously, I collaborated with [Dr. Mahmud Hasan](https://egr.vcu.edu/directory/mahmud.hasan/) (Virginia Commonwealth University) on client selection for federated learning under extreme statistical and system heterogeneity, and conducted undergraduate research at Jahangirnagar University under [Dr. Mohammad Abu Yousuf](https://www.juniv.edu/teacher/mayousuf) in biomedical AI and quantum machine learning.
